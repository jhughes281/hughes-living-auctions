Bench protection

Optional coverage you can add on the bid sheet for grades A through C. It covers our repair, for ninety days. Here is the entire policy.

Covered for 90 days

  • The fault we repaired, if it comes back. Same seam, same mechanism, same joint.
  • Reclining, lifting and swivel mechanisms on any lot we serviced.
  • Electrical work we performed, including rewired lamps and power units.
  • Joinery we glued, doweled or re-drilled.
  • Your choice when we cannot fix it twice: full refund or an equal lot from an open pallet.

Not covered, ever

  • Anything printed on the “still” line of the tag. You bought that flaw on purpose.
  • Stains, tears and pet damage that happen after pickup.
  • Damage in your vehicle or in your doorway. Measure first.
  • Grade D lots. Those are sold as parts and cannot be covered.
  • Normal wear, and any repair made by someone other than our bench.

Protection runs 6% of the hammer price, $8 minimum, added at checkout. On a $260 sectional that is $15.60. Decline it and nothing else about the lot changes — there is no second price.

Bidding and pickup

Proxy bidding, published increments, and a clock that will not let anyone steal a lot in the last three seconds.

Bid your maximum, not your next step

Enter the most you would pay. We bid the smallest amount needed to keep you in front, and stop at your number. Nobody sees your maximum, including us, until it is beaten.

The two-minute rule

Any bid inside the final two minutes pushes the close out by two more. A lot ends when two full minutes pass with nobody willing to go higher. Sniping does not work here.

Buy it now until the first bid

Some lots carry a buy-it-now price. It stays live until somebody bids, then it comes off and the lot runs to the clock.

Pay at close, pick up within four days

Card on file is charged when the clock stops. Bring help and a truck — we load, you strap. Local delivery is quoted per lot before you bid; ask on the lot page.
